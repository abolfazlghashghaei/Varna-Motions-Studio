[Script Installation]

1. Copy the `Highlighter.jsxbin` file to the After Effects scripts directory:

Win: ...Adobe After Effects CC\Support Files\Scripts\ScriptUI Panels\
Mac: Applications/Adobe After Effects CC/Scripts/ScriptUI Panels/


2. Open the After Effects preferences and enable the option "Allow Scripts to Write Files and Access Network":
Win: Edit > Preferences > General
Mac: After Effects CC > Preferences > General


3. You will find the `Highlighter.jsxbin` script listed under the "Window" menu at the top of the application.
